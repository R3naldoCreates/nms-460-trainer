      ALGIAMED NMS 460

      Training simulator

      An interactive practice environment for the Algiamed NMS 460 peripheral nerve stimulator. It reproduces the device's screen, menus, adjustment wheel and treatment workflow so clinicians can rehearse before touching a real unit.

      • Not a medical device. No output is generated. Nothing here is for diagnosis or treatment.
      • The comfort meter and fasciculation response are simulated patient models, not measurements.
      • Always follow the current Instructions for Use and your local protocols with real equipment.
